Privacy Policy

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit usa.teduza.com. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

2. What this website does not do

  • It does not set cookies and does not use local browser storage.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not embed third-party content, and fonts are served from this website itself.
  • It contains no forms; no data is collected through the pages.

3. Server log data

When you open a page, the hosting provider that delivers this website may automatically process technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of access, the requested address and information about your browser. This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure.

For visitors in the European Union, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Contacting us by e-mail

If you write to us, we process your message and contact details only to respond to your enquiry and for any follow-up correspondence. We do not pass them on to third parties for marketing.

5. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to access, rectify or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Visitors in the European Union may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, contact us at the e-mail address above.

6. Changes

We will update this policy if the website changes in a way that affects personal data, for example if analytics or a contact form are introduced.
