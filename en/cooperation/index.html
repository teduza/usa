Cooperation

We are open to dialogue with organisations in the United States that share an interest in personal, privacy-respecting AI.

Who we would like to hear from

01

Research institutions

Groups working on reasoning, human–AI interaction or privacy who see a fit with our areas of interest.

02

Universities

Faculties and departments interested in academic dialogue, joint questions or student projects.

03

Companies

Technology and industry partners with a related interest in personal or offline AI.

04

Public-sector organisations

Institutions interested in responsible, privacy-respecting AI for people.

How a conversation starts

  1. Initial contactWrite to us with a short description of your organisation and the topic you have in mind.
  2. Introductory callWe arrange a conversation to understand mutual interests.
  3. ConfidentialityWhere confidential information is involved, we agree a non-disclosure framework first.
  4. Defined scopeAny cooperation is based on a written agreement with a clear scope.
