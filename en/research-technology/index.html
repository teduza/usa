Research & Technology

Our R&D focuses on one question: what it takes to build a personal AI companion that works offline and stays consistent over time.

Areas of interest

01

Offline reasoning

Approaches that allow useful reasoning on personal devices with limited resources and without constant connectivity.

02

Continuity and personality

How a digital companion can maintain a coherent character and context over long periods of use.

03

Human–AI interaction

Trust, transparency and healthy boundaries in long-term interaction between a person and an AI companion.

04

Privacy-preserving design

Design choices that keep personal information under the control of the person it belongs to.

Researchers in the United States

We welcome contact from universities and research institutions in the United States working in related fields. No research collaboration in the United States has been established to date.
