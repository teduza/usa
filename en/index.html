At a glance

01

What M.A.R.S. is

M.A.R.S. (Mobile Autonomous Reasoning System) is a personal AI companion and digital personality project designed to work without depending on the cloud.

02

Who is behind it

M.A.R.S. COMPANION LLC, a company incorporated in the Republic of Armenia. Its founder and director is Aleksandr Sarkisian.

03

Connection status

M.A.R.S. USA is the national website and point of dialogue for the United States. Status: establishing connection.

Why the United States

The United States is home to one of the world's largest technology and research ecosystems. It is a priority region for the international development of M.A.R.S. COMPANION LLC and a key place for dialogue about the future of personal AI.

Part of a global presence

Founded in Kapan, Armenia, M.A.R.S. COMPANION LLC is developing a presence in the European Union and the United States.
