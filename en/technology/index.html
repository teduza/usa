M.A.R.S. technology

M.A.R.S. — Mobile Autonomous Reasoning System — is an offline personal AI companion and digital personality project developed by M.A.R.S. COMPANION LLC.

Design principles

01

Offline by design

M.A.R.S. is designed to run locally on the user's own hardware, so personal interaction does not depend on remote servers.

02

A personal voice companion

It is built for spoken conversation with one person over the long term, rather than anonymous one-off queries.

03

Long-term memory and personality

M.A.R.S. is conceived as a digital personality with a stable character and long-term memory across conversations.

04

Privacy as a starting point

No mandatory accounts, subscriptions or remote data collection. What a person shares stays with them.

Development status

M.A.R.S. is under active development. Product specifications, availability and release dates have not been published.

We will describe capabilities publicly once they can be demonstrated, and not before.

What we do not publish

Technical architecture, unpublished research and work related to intellectual property are confidential. Detailed technical discussions take place only within a formal framework, such as a non-disclosure agreement.
